# P2-S2 Final Answer Evaluation V2

`eval/answer_scoring.py` is a separate, local scorer; it imports Eval contracts only, no Agent, provider or business executor. Inputs are final public answer, explicit typed Gold, already-recorded tool events/retrieved chunks, and independently computed tool/environment results. Nothing passes Gold into service/provider prompts. Historical tool/state scorers remain unchanged. New metrics cannot be compared directly to old tool-only success.

AnswerGold declares independent subgoals, subject-bound acceptable fact patterns, prohibited claims, and required actual tool/retrieval field assertions. Alternative language is accepted through annotated patterns, not whole-answer exact matching. Tool evidence must be successful, nontruncated, executed/replayed and satisfy arguments plus typed field values. Missing fields differ from explicit null. Negated facts do not satisfy positive assertions. Fact/goal coverage and evidence support are scored independently; a fluid answer without required actual tool execution fails overall.

Metrics: TOOL_SUCCESS, ENVIRONMENT_SUCCESS, ANSWER_FACTUAL_CORRECTNESS, ANSWER_COMPLETENESS, ANSWER_FAITHFULNESS, ABSTENTION_CORRECTNESS, OVERALL_TASK_SUCCESS, JUDGE_VALIDITY. Missing required fact/goal, contradiction, failed tool/state or unsupported annotated fact causes failure. Matched assertions alone do not certify every sentence: unmatched clauses, unannotated goals, policy without verified retrieval and required open semantics remain NOT_ASSESSED. A wholly verified closed annotated contract can pass deterministic end-to-end evaluation; this is not a general-purpose human-quality score. Clear abstention is evaluated against its own Gold and cannot excuse unrelated unverified claims.

Initial RED11tests → GREEN11; additional unknown-promise and static-policy checks yield13passing. Independent review supplied five actual counterexamples (one S1 amount issue and four evaluator issues): another entity's duplicated status was erased, truncated evidence accepted, absent field matched null, and abstention ignored extra unsupported text. Corrected with bound match spans, known-entity scope, invalid-evidence filtering, missing sentinel and shared abstention uncertainty gates. Joint review/scorer/answer tests38pass. Review findings and regrade are retained; this is independent engineering review, not human semantic annotation.

No paid semantic judge used. No human calibration has been performed (NOT_RUN); unconstrained natural-language semantics remain NOT_ASSESSED when deterministic coverage is insufficient. Same-model self-judgments from the product are never used as independent Gold truth. The evaluator is intentionally conservative: legitimate paraphrases outside annotated expressions may remain unknown, and this limitation must be reported rather than hidden by relabeling Gold.

New datasets freeze this contract and its SHA before final holdout use. Existing historical datasets have no answer Gold, so their answer metrics must remain NOT_ASSESSED unless a separately identified new annotation version is created; original tool/state results remain intact.
